The interface of Studio.

- Open folder: pick `.boardy/` or the project root; it auto-discovers `.boardy/index.md` and indexes if needed, and ensures `.boardy/.gitignore`.
- Graph view: nodes colored by layer; click a node to expand its links.
- Command palette: `Cmd-K` for fast navigation and commands.
- Search: fast full-text search.
- Note editor: click a node to open a floating panel on the right and edit in place.
- AI assistant: configured once; suggests links, flags misplaced notes, and summarizes.
